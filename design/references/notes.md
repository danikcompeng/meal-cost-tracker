## Things to look for (my app's main screens)

- **Budget bar / big totals:** how they show one important number (Copilot Money, Apple Health)
- **Lists:** spacing between rows, what's bold vs grey (Linear, Things 3)
- **Forms on a phone:** entering a price + package size quickly (Stripe)
- **One accent color:** where it's used, and where it isn't
